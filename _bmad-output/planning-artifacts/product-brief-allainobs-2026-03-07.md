# Product Brief: allainobs.com

## Executive Summary

allainobs.com ("All AI, No BS") is a consulting and education platform operated by Jarad DeLorenzo, a Staff Engineer with 25+ years of production engineering experience and deep expertise in agentic systems, AI workflow automation, and multi-agent orchestration. The platform converts Jarad's technical authority into a tiered service business that helps teams, businesses, and individual professionals integrate AI workflows into their day-to-day operations. The core funnel moves prospects from free bi-weekly live webinars through paid small-cohort workshops to premium 1-on-1 consulting sessions.

---

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

Teams and businesses know they need to integrate AI into their workflows but are overwhelmed by hype, conflicting advice, and tools that promise transformation but deliver complexity. Most AI education content is either too theoretical (slides and frameworks with no real-world application) or too shallow (prompt tips that don't scale). Leaders can't distinguish between genuine productivity gains and marketing noise, resulting in wasted budgets, failed pilots, and growing organizational anxiety about falling behind.

### Problem Impact

- Teams waste months evaluating AI tools without clear integration strategies
- Businesses invest in AI initiatives that fail to deliver measurable ROI
- Individual contributors feel pressure to "use AI" without understanding how it fits their specific workflows
- Decision makers can't evaluate AI vendor claims or assess technical feasibility
- Failed AI initiatives create organizational skepticism that blocks future adoption

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

- **Generic AI courses** (Coursera, Udemy): Theory-heavy, not tailored to specific business contexts, no live interaction or accountability
- **AI consulting firms** (McKinsey, Deloitte): Enterprise-only pricing, strategy decks without hands-on implementation, months-long engagements
- **YouTube/blog content**: Fragmented, often outdated, no feedback loop, heavy on hype
- **Vendor-specific training**: Locked to one tool, biased toward that vendor's ecosystem
- **AI bootcamps**: Developer-focused, not accessible to business teams, don't address organizational adoption

None of these combine hands-on live instruction, small cohort intimacy, practical workflow building, and the credibility of someone who has shipped AI systems at companies like Warby Parker, ClassPass, Chase Bank, and BAE Systems.

### Proposed Solution

A three-tier service platform that meets clients where they are:

1. **Free Bi-Weekly Webinar** (Cloudflare Live Stream): Live demos of real AI workflows, agent systems, and automation patterns. No slides, no sales pitch. Builds audience, establishes authority, and naturally identifies warm leads.

2. **Paid Workshops** (Cloudflare Stream, max 8 per cohort): Intensive, hands-on sessions where participants build AI workflows tailored to their actual work. Both public cohorts and custom team workshops available. Recorded for lifetime access.

3. **1-on-1 Consulting** (45 minutes): Premium, personalized deep-dives into architecture decisions, workflow design, agent coordination, integration strategy, or team adoption challenges. Clients leave with a concrete action plan.

### Key Differentiators

- **Practitioner credibility**: 25 years of shipping production systems at recognizable brands, not a career consultant
- **Live, hands-on format**: Build real workflows during sessions, not passive lectures
- **Small cohort model**: Max 8 per workshop ensures personalized attention and real problem-solving
- **Tool-agnostic approach**: Focus on patterns and architecture, not vendor lock-in
- **"All AI, No BS" positioning**: Cuts through hype, resonates with engineers and technical leaders who are skeptical of AI marketing

## Target Users

### Primary Users

**1. "Sarah the Engineering Manager"**
Mid-level engineering manager at a 50-500 person company. Her CTO is pushing AI adoption but nobody on her team has hands-on experience integrating AI into existing workflows. She's evaluated 3 AI tools, run a failed pilot, and is under pressure to show results next quarter. She needs practical, proven patterns she can implement with her team, not more vendor demos.

- **Motivation**: Prove AI value to leadership, upskill her team, reduce manual toil
- **Pain**: Information overload, failed pilots, can't distinguish hype from substance
- **Journey**: Discovers webinar via LinkedIn -> attends 2-3 free sessions -> enrolls team in custom workshop -> books 1-on-1 for architecture review

**2. "Marcus the Founder/CTO"**
Technical founder of a Series A/B startup. Building product fast and knows AI could accelerate development but doesn't have time to research the landscape. Needs someone who's been there to cut through the noise and give him an actionable integration strategy in one session.

- **Motivation**: Ship faster, competitive advantage, efficient team scaling
- **Pain**: Time-constrained, can't afford wrong AI bets, needs expert shortcut
- **Journey**: Referred by peer or finds via content -> books 1-on-1 directly -> may enroll team in workshop afterward

**3. "Diana the Individual Contributor"**
Senior developer or technical lead who wants to level up on AI/agent patterns. Self-motivated learner who watches the free webinars and eventually invests in a workshop to go deeper with structured guidance.

- **Motivation**: Career growth, practical AI skills, build portfolio of AI-integrated projects
- **Pain**: Existing courses are too basic or too theoretical, wants hands-on building
- **Journey**: Discovers webinar via Twitter/blog -> becomes regular attendee -> joins public workshop cohort

### Secondary Users

- **HR/L&D leads** looking for AI training programs for technical teams
- **Non-technical executives** seeking AI strategy briefings (1-on-1 track)
- **Agency owners** wanting to integrate AI into client deliverables

### User Journey

1. **Discovery**: LinkedIn content, Twitter, webinar promotion, peer referral, SEO
2. **First Touch**: Free bi-weekly webinar (low commitment, high value)
3. **Engagement**: Email list, webinar replays, free content library
4. **Conversion**: Workshop enrollment or 1-on-1 booking
5. **Retention**: Workshop recordings, ongoing webinar community, repeat consulting
6. **Advocacy**: Alumni referrals, testimonials, case studies

## MVP Scope

### Core Features

1. **Landing Page**: Hero with value prop, client marquee (ClassPass, Warby Parker, etc.), service tiers, CTAs
2. **Webinar Section**: Next session countdown, email signup for notifications, embed for Cloudflare Live Stream
3. **Workshop Section**: Service descriptions, pricing, Stripe checkout for cohort enrollment, waitlist functionality
4. **1-on-1 Booking**: Calendly (or similar) embed for 45-minute session scheduling
5. **Email Capture**: Newsletter signup integrated across all sections
6. **Video Content Library**: Grid of free + premium videos, Cloudflare Stream player, Stripe-gated access for premium content
7. **Basic SEO**: Meta tags, Open Graph, sitemap, structured data

### Out of Scope for MVP

- User accounts/authentication (use Stripe customer portal + email for access)
- Blog/CMS (add post-launch, start with static content)
- Community features (Discord/Slack integration)
- Course completion tracking or certificates
- Mobile app
- Affiliate/referral program
- Multi-instructor support

### MVP Success Criteria

- Site is live on allainobs.com via Cloudflare Pages
- Webinar signup captures emails and sends notifications
- At least one workshop cohort can be purchased via Stripe
- 1-on-1 sessions are bookable
- Premium video content is gated behind payment
- Client marquee scrolls with all 11 company logos/names
- Page loads in under 2 seconds on mobile
